# Dongguan Sweater Manufacturer Guide: Understanding the Dalang Knitwear Industry

Dalang produces roughly **1 out of every 5 sweaters globally**. The true competitive advantage for B2B buyers lies in its **20km supply chain density**—integrating yarn markets, dye houses, sample rooms, and export logistics.

---

## Supply Chain Snapshot

- **Enterprises:** 28,000+ active textile factories and workshops
- **Machinery:** ~150,000 computerized flat knitting machines (80%+ domestic seamless tech)
- **Yarn Velocity:** $5B USD annual trade volume with 10,000+ new seasonal yarn varieties

---

## Factory Landscape: Supplier Matrix

### Tier 1: Vertically Integrated Giants
- **Target MOQ:** 1,000+ pcs/color | **Lead Time:** 30–45 days
- **Best For:** High-volume global brands requiring full compliance (BSCI, ISO 9001).

### Tier 2: Mid-Size OEM/ODM Specialists
- **Target MOQ:** 300–500 pcs/color | **Lead Time:** 20–30 days
- **Best For:** Designer labels needing fast prototyping and complex stitches (Jacquard, Intarsia, Cable).

### Tier 3: Small-Batch Agile Workshops
- **Target MOQ:** 30–100 pcs/color | **Lead Time:** 7–12 days
- **Best For:** DTC e-commerce brands, capsule lines, and rapid style testing.
